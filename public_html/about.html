Frequently Asked Questions

📌 What is a DSC?
A Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) is an encrypted signature used to authenticate identity online for filing ITR, MCA forms, GST, tenders, etc.

📌 Why do I need a DSC?
DSC is mandatory for legal and secure filing with government portals like MCA, GST, Income Tax, eProcurement, etc.

📌 How fast can I get my DSC?
We issue DSCs within 30 to 60 minutes post-KYC. It’s 100% paperless and hassle-free.

📌 Is this service authorized?
Yes. We are certified partners of eMudhra, VSign, Sify, and Capricorn, licensed by the CCA, Govt. of India.

📌 What documents are needed?
PAN Card, Aadhaar (linked to mobile), and a passport-size photo are all that’s required.

📌 What types of DSCs do you offer?
Class 3 DSC (Individual), DGFT DSC, Document Signer, and USB Tokens.

📌 Is Aadhaar-based eKYC valid?
Yes. Aadhaar-linked mobile verification enables eKYC over OTP and video call — totally paperless.

📌 What is a DGFT DSC used for?
It's used by EXIM businesses to access DGFT portal services, apply for licenses, and make filings.

📌 Do you deliver USB Tokens?
Yes, we provide PAN India delivery with same-day dispatch. Tracking is shared via WhatsApp.

📌 Can I renew an expired DSC?
Absolutely. We offer renewal at discounted pricing before and after expiry.

📌 How do I install the DSC?
We guide you step-by-step via WhatsApp or remote support. Installation is easy on Windows & Mac.

📌 Is support available after purchase?
Yes, we offer 24x7 support via WhatsApp, call, and email.

📌 Can a company apply for employee DSCs?
Yes. We provide bulk DSC issuance and custom invoices for organizations.

📌 What is the price for Class 3 DSC?
₹1300 standard. We offer discounts up to ₹900 with combo plans. Check our pricing section.

📌 What is Document Signer DSC?
It's used to auto-sign PDFs/forms in bulk on behalf of organizations — ideal for ERPs, bulk communication.

📌 Can you issue DSC outside Delhi?
Yes! We serve customers from all over India — Patna, Kolkata, Bengaluru, Bokaro, Mumbai, and more.

📌 Are your tokens secure?
We use govt-approved FIPS-certified USB Tokens like ProxKey, mToken, and HYP2003.

📌 Do you offer combo discounts?
Yes, we offer combo pricing for DSC + Token bundles — great savings for first-time buyers.

📌 Is video verification required?
Yes, as per CCA norms, a quick selfie/video call is required. Takes just 2 minutes.

📌 How do I contact you instantly?
Call or WhatsApp us directly from the website header — we’re online 24x7!
