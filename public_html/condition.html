📜 Terms & Conditions, Refund Policy & Privacy

1. Terms & Conditions

  • All digital certificates are issued after completing KYC and verification as per government norms.
  • Orders placed are non-transferable and linked to the verified identity only.
  • Customer must ensure correct information is submitted during application.
  • Misuse of DSC is legally punishable under the IT Act.
  • EasySign reserves the right to refuse or cancel any suspicious orders.
  • Pricing and offers may change without prior notice.

2. Return & Refund Policy

  • Digital Signature Certificates (DSC) are non-returnable and non-refundable once issued.
  • If the customer applies and changes their mind before DSC processing, a partial refund may be granted.
  • No refund will be issued for incorrect details or rejected applications by Certifying Authorities.
  • USB Tokens can be returned within 7 days only if unopened and unused, with original packaging.
  • Refunds (if applicable) will be processed within 5–7 business days.

3. Privacy Policy

  • We collect your name, email, phone number, and documents only for DSC application purposes.
  • Your information is kept strictly confidential and shared only with licensed Certifying Authorities.
  • We do not sell or misuse your data under any circumstances.
  • Our site uses secure protocols (HTTPS) for safe data transmission.
  • For queries or data deletion requests, contact: easysignservices@gmail.com
